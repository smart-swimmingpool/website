## 📋 Project Overview

The **Smart Swimming Pool** project is a modular, open-source system for automating your swimming pool. The core module is the **Pool Controller**, an ESP32-based device that:

- ✅ Automates circulation time for cleaning water
- ✅ Controls heating with solar energy
- ✅ Manages water heating via additional pump for heating circuit
- ✅ Works independently of specific smart home servers
- ✅ Integrates seamlessly with Home Assistant via MQTT Discovery
- ✅ Works without permanent WiFi connection
- ✅ Can be operated via smartphone or hardware buttons
- ✅ Is modular and extensible
