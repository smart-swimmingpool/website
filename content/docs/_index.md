## \ud83d\udcc Project Overview

The **Smart Swimming Pool** project is a modular, open-source system for automating your swimming pool. The core module is the **Pool Controller**, an ESP32-based device that:

- \u2705 Automates circulation time for cleaning water
- \u2705 Controls heating with solar energy
- \u2705 Manages water heating via additional pump for heating circuit
- \u2705 Works independently of specific smart home servers
- \u2705 Integrates seamlessly with Home Assistant via MQTT Discovery
- \u2705 Works without permanent WiFi connection
- \u2705 Can be operated via smartphone or hardware buttons
- \u2705 Is modular and extensible
